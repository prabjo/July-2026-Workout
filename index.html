What Goes in the Bag

  • Resistance band — replaces every cable machine a hotel gym doesn't have
  • Lacrosse ball, or use a golf ball from your bag
  • Running shoes — the one non-negotiable, hotel treadmills are fine but their loaner shoes aren't
  • Shaker bottle + 3–4 servings of protein powder in a ziplock
  • Jerky or protein bars for the Thursday travel day
💡 A band and a pair of shoes turns any hotel room into a workable gym. Everything in the travel week above runs on dumbbells, a treadmill, or nothing at all.
