Smoothie

  • 1 cup plain Greek yogurt
  • 1 scoop protein powder
  • 1–2 tbsp chia seeds
  • 1 tbsp ground flaxseed
  • 1 apple
  • 1/2 cup frozen mango
  • 1 handful spinach
  • Fresh ginger
  • Water, milk, or kefir to blend
Protein: ~35–45g
Fiber: ~12–18g
Fermented: Yes if yogurt/kefir has live cultures

Overnight Oats

  • 1/2 cup oats
  • 1 cup Greek yogurt
  • 1 tbsp chia seeds
  • 1 tbsp ground flaxseed
  • 1/2 to 1 cup berries
  • Optional diced apple
  • Splash of milk or kefir if needed
Protein: ~28–32g
Fiber: ~12–16g
Fermented: Yes

Salmon Dinner

  • 5–6 oz salmon
  • 1 cup kale or broccolini
  • 4 mini potatoes, skin on
  • 1 tbsp miso on the side or in broth
Protein: ~45–55g
Fiber: ~8–11g
Fermented: Yes from miso

Breakfast Formula

  • Pick 1 protein anchor: 3 eggs or Greek yogurt
  • Add fiber: chia, flax, oats, berries, apple
  • Add fermented: yogurt or kefir
  • Target 30–40g protein

Lunch Formula

  • Protein: chicken, dal, black beans, turkey, or protein shake if needed
  • Veg: okra, greens, beets
  • Carb: roti or potatoes
  • Fermented side: yogurt, kefir, kimchi

Dinner Formula

  • Protein: salmon, eggs, dal, or chicken
  • Veg: kale, broccolini, okra, gobi
  • Carb: potatoes or roti as needed
  • Fermented side: miso, yogurt, kefir

Snack Ideas

  • Greek yogurt + fruit
  • Fruit + peanut butter
  • Fruit + nuts
  • Kefir if you need protein + fermented support
