How to Build One

  • Smoothie or bowl carries the fiber and the fermented serving
  • 2 eggs on the side carry the last 12g of protein
  • One fruit, not three — the apple is enough sweetness
  • Add fat: nut butter, avocado, or hemp
  • Target 45–50g protein, 18–25g fiber
💡 Every option below lands in the same range. Rotate them so you're not eating the same three plants every morning.
Option 1 · Blender

Green Machine + 2 Eggs

Your standing recipe, tuned. Makes 2 portions — numbers below are your half.

Smoothie (makes 2)
  • 2 cups plain Greek yogurt
  • 1 scoop protein powder
  • 1 avocado
  • 2 apples
  • 1 handful frozen mango (drop the banana — one fruit sugar source is plenty)
  • 2 tbsp chia seeds
  • 2 tbsp ground flaxseed
  • 2 tbsp peanut or almond butter
  • 2 handfuls spinach
  • Fresh ginger, skin on
  • Cinnamon
  • Coconut water to blend
On the side
  • 2 eggs, any style
Protein: ~50g
Fiber: ~24g
Fermented: Yes
💡 Celery is optional filler — spinach earns its spot, celery mostly adds water.
Option 2 · Blender

Berry Cottage + 2 Eggs

Higher protein, lower sugar. Best on lift days.

Smoothie (single serving)
  • 1 cup cottage cheese (28g — beats yogurt by 8g)
  • 1 scoop protein powder
  • 1 cup frozen blueberries
  • 3 tbsp hemp hearts
  • 1 tbsp chia seeds
  • 1 handful spinach
  • Cinnamon
  • Kefir or milk to blend
On the side
  • 2 eggs
Protein: ~62g
Fiber: ~18g
Fermented: Yes ×2
💡 Cottage cheese and kefir are different microbial families — this one covers two fermented servings in a single glass.
Option 3 · Stovetop

Oatmeal Bowl + 2 Eggs

The no-blender day. Near parity with the smoothies.

  • 1/2 cup rolled oats
  • 1 cup Greek yogurt, stirred in after cooking
  • 1 tbsp chia seeds
  • 1 tbsp ground flaxseed
  • 1 cup blueberries + diced apple
  • Handful spinach stirred into the warm oats (replaces the smoothie's greens — you won't taste it)
  • 1 tbsp peanut butter
  • Cinnamon, drizzle of raw honey
  • 2 eggs on the side
Protein: ~45g
Fiber: ~20g
Fermented: Yes
💡 Without the spinach and flax you lose the greens and one omega-3 source. Those two additions are what make this equal to the smoothie.
Option 4 · Make-Ahead

Overnight Oats + Hard-Boiled Eggs

Built the night before. For mornings that get away from you.

  • 1/2 cup oats
  • 1 cup Greek yogurt
  • 1 scoop protein powder
  • 1 tbsp chia seeds
  • 1 tbsp ground flaxseed
  • 1 cup frozen blueberries (thaw overnight in the jar)
  • Splash of kefir
  • 2 hard-boiled eggs from the fridge batch
Protein: ~55g
Fiber: ~18g
Fermented: Yes ×2
💡 Boil six eggs Sunday. This is the option that keeps a rushed morning from becoming a 15g breakfast.
Option 5 · No Prep

Savory Bowl

When you don't want anything sweet.

  • 1 cup cottage cheese
  • 3 eggs, scrambled or fried
  • 1/2 avocado, sliced
  • 1/2 cup kimchi
  • Handful spinach, wilted in the egg pan
  • Olive oil, salt, pepper
  • Whole grain toast if you want the carb
Protein: ~48g
Fiber: ~11g
Fermented: Yes ×2
💡 Lowest fiber of the five — make up for it at lunch with beans or lentils.

Vitamix Loading Order

  • 1 · Liquid — coconut water, milk, or kefir
  • 2 · Yogurt or cottage cheese
  • 3 · Powder, ginger, nut butter
  • 4 · Spinach
  • 5 · Apple and avocado
  • 6 · Frozen fruit on top — weight pushes everything down
  • 7 · Chia and flax last, final 10 seconds
💡 Start on variable 1, catch the vortex, then ramp to 10 over five seconds. Slamming to high is what causes the air pocket. Chia and flax gel fast and thicken the vortex — add them at the end.

Lunch — The One That Needs Work

  • 6 oz chicken (not "a few pieces")
  • 1 cup lentils, white beans, or black chickpeas
  • Brown rice or roti
  • Veg — okra, greens, beets
  • 1/2 cup kimchi on the side
  • Olive oil or tahini for fat
Protein: ~60g
Fiber: ~18g
Fermented: Yes
💡 The single biggest fix. Making lunch the largest protein meal closes most of the daily gap on its own.

Salmon Dinner

  • 5–6 oz salmon — salt 20 min ahead, pull at 120–125°F
  • 1 cup kale or broccolini
  • Roasted sweet potato or 4 mini potatoes, skin on
  • Lentils underneath, or white beans tossed with the greens
  • 1 tbsp miso on the side or in broth
  • Beets — roast whole with skin on, rub the skin off after
Protein: ~50–55g
Fiber: ~14–16g
Fermented: Yes from miso

Chickpea Pasta Bowl

  • 2 oz chickpea pasta
  • Pesto
  • 6 oz chicken or 2 eggs on top (the version without this lands at 22g — too low)
  • 1 cup broccoli or broccolini
  • 1/2 avocado
  • Kraut or kimchi on the side
Protein: ~50g
Fiber: ~22g
Fermented: Yes

Rice + Black Chickpea Bowl

  • 3/4 cup brown rice
  • 1 cup black chickpeas
  • Second protein — chicken, tempeh, or 2 eggs
  • Roasted veg
  • Olive oil or tahini
  • Kimchi
Protein: ~45g
Fiber: ~20g
Fermented: Yes

Breakfast Formula

  • Base: Greek yogurt, cottage cheese, or oats
  • Always add 2 eggs — that's the last 12g
  • Fiber: chia, hemp, flax, berries, apple
  • Greens: spinach, blended or wilted
  • Fat: nut butter, avocado, or hemp
  • Target 45–50g protein

Lunch Formula

  • Protein: 6 oz chicken, or dal plus a second source
  • Second protein: beans, lentils, cottage cheese, or eggs
  • Veg: okra, greens, beets
  • Carb: roti, brown rice, or potatoes
  • Fermented side: yogurt, kefir, kimchi
  • Target 45–50g protein — this is the meal that's been short

Dinner Formula

  • Protein: salmon, chicken, rib eye, or dal
  • If the base is plant-only, add a second source — eggs, sardines, or leftover chicken
  • Veg: kale, broccolini, okra, gobi
  • Carb: sweet potato, potatoes, or roti
  • Fermented side: miso, kraut, kimchi, yogurt
  • Target 40–45g protein

Snack Ideas

  • Cottage cheese + berries — 28g, zero prep
  • Hard-boiled eggs — keep a batch in the fridge
  • Kefir — protein and fermented in one
  • Greek yogurt + fruit
  • Nuts or fruit + nut butter — for calories on golf days
  • Jerky or a shake at the turn

Fermented — Rotate the Type

  • Dairy: yogurt, kefir — different strains than vegetable ferments
  • Vegetable: kimchi, sauerkraut
  • Soy: miso, tempeh
  • Aim for two different families per day, not three servings of the same one
