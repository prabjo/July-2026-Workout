Travel-week training · room workouts · hotel gym options · run fallback

Travel Week Rules

  • Keep the same weekly pattern as your regular plan.
  • Each day has a short 10–15 min morning room session.
  • Each day also has a main session later if time and energy allow.
  • Monday and Thursday are built as travel-bridge days.
  • If you are home by Thursday, switch back to your normal Thursday strength day.
  • If you are home Friday/Saturday/Sunday, your normal home workouts still apply.
